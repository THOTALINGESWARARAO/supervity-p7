ACME TECHNOLOGIES
EMPLOYEE BENEFITS POLICY

Document Type: HR Policy
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. HEALTH INSURANCE ELIGIBILITY

All full-time employees are eligible for the company health insurance plan from their date of joining.

2. HEALTH INSURANCE ENROLLMENT

New employees must enroll in the health insurance plan within 30 calendar days of their joining date.

3. PREMIUM CONTRIBUTION

Acme Technologies covers 80% of the employee's health insurance premium. The employee is responsible for the remaining 20%.

4. DEPENDENT COVERAGE

Employees may add eligible dependents to their health insurance plan during the enrollment process. Additional dependent premiums are paid by the employee unless otherwise specified by HR.

5. DENTAL AND VISION BENEFITS

Full-time employees are eligible for basic dental and vision coverage after completing the benefits enrollment process.

6. BENEFITS SUPPORT

Employees who have questions about benefits should contact the HR team through the internal HR help desk.

7. IMPORTANT LIMITATION

This document describes the standard employee benefits policy. It does not contain information about specialized international benefits, executive benefits, or temporary contractor benefits.
