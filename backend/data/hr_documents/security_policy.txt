ACME TECHNOLOGIES
INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY

Document Type: Security Policy
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. SECURITY TRAINING

All new employees must complete the mandatory security awareness training within 7 calendar days of their joining date.

2. PASSWORDS

Employees must use strong, unique passwords for company accounts.

Passwords must not be shared with colleagues or other individuals.

3. MULTI-FACTOR AUTHENTICATION

MFA is required for company accounts and supported internal applications.

Employees must not disable MFA unless specifically instructed by authorized IT personnel.

4. PHISHING

Employees should not click suspicious links, open unexpected attachments, or provide company credentials in response to suspicious messages.

Potential phishing attempts should be reported to the IT or security team.

5. COMPANY DEVICES

Company-managed devices must be protected from unauthorized access.

Employees should lock their device when leaving it unattended.

6. SECURITY INCIDENTS

Suspected security incidents must be reported to the security or IT team as soon as possible.

7. DATA PROTECTION

Employees must handle company and customer information according to applicable company policies and access controls.

8. POLICY LIMITATION

This document describes general employee information-security requirements. It does not provide detailed procedures for every security incident or system.
