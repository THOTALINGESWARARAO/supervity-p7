ACME TECHNOLOGIES
NEW EMPLOYEE IT SETUP GUIDE

Document Type: IT Onboarding Guide
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. COMPANY LAPTOP

New employees receive a company-managed laptop from the IT department.

Employees should sign in using their company credentials during the initial setup.

2. COMPANY EMAIL

A company email account is created before the employee's start date.

The employee should verify access to email during the first day of onboarding.

3. MULTI-FACTOR AUTHENTICATION

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) must be enabled on the employee's company account.

Employees should complete MFA setup before accessing internal company applications.

4. VPN

Employees working remotely must use the company VPN when accessing systems that require internal network access.

The IT department provides VPN configuration instructions during onboarding.

5. SOFTWARE

Standard employee laptops include the applications required for email, communication, documentation, and approved development work.

Employees must not install unapproved software on company-managed devices.

6. IT SUPPORT

For laptop, account, VPN, or software issues, employees should contact the IT help desk.

7. LOST OR STOLEN DEVICES

A lost or stolen company device must be reported to the IT help desk immediately.

Employees should not attempt to recover or remotely modify a lost device without following IT instructions.
