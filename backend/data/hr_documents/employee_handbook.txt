ACME TECHNOLOGIES
EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK

Document Type: Employee Handbook
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. WORKING HOURS

Standard working hours are Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM local time.

Employees normally receive a one-hour lunch break.

2. REMOTE WORK

Eligible employees may work remotely according to their team's approved work arrangement.

Remote work eligibility does not automatically authorize working from another country.

International remote work requires separate approval from HR and the employee's manager.

3. PAID TIME OFF

Full-time employees receive paid time off according to the company's applicable leave policy.

Employees should request planned leave through the company's designated leave-management process.

4. SICK LEAVE

Employees who are unable to work because of illness should notify their manager as soon as reasonably possible.

5. EXPENSES

Business expenses must be submitted through the company's expense process with appropriate receipts or supporting documentation.

6. CODE OF CONDUCT

Employees are expected to communicate professionally, respect colleagues, protect confidential company information, and comply with company policies.

7. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

Employees must not disclose confidential company information to unauthorized individuals.

8. HR QUESTIONS

Employees should contact HR when they need clarification about employment policies, benefits, leave, or workplace matters.
